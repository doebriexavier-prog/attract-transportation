Airport Shuttle Coasters

Our special airport buses can accommodate up to 25 passengers and are designed with extra-large luggage space, making them ideal for airport transfers and groups traveling with large amounts of baggage.

Please note: These buses are available for extra-luggage trips. Route availability may be limited.

  • Clean & well-maintained buses
  • Air-conditioned
  • Drink coolers
  • Sun-blocking curtains
  • Extra-large luggage space
Capacity 10–29 passengers

Toyota Coasters

We offer different Toyota Coaster options to match your needs, from Super Lounges with large reclining seats to models with easy-access rear doors for larger carry-on items.

Features vary by bus and passenger count. For maximum comfort, we recommend 20–25 passengers. At 29 passengers, foldable seats may be required and some comfort features, such as a cooler, may not be available.

For the best experience, request our Super Lounge, with a maximum capacity of 18 passengers.

  • Clean & well-maintained buses
  • Air-conditioned
  • Comfortable seating
Capacity 5-15 passengers

Hiace

Our HiAce buses are ideal for smaller groups, quick pickups, and both short and long-distance trips. They can accommodate up to 15 passengers and are a great choice when you need a compact and convenient transportation option.

For the best comfort and luggage space, we recommend around 10 passengers or fewer, leaving plenty of room for luggage, bags, and other items.

  • Small group travel
  • Clean & well-maintained buses
  • Air-conditioned
Capacity 10–20 passengers

4 Wheel Drive

Our 4WD Mitsubishi Rosa buses are built to take you where regular buses can’t. Designed for rough terrain and remote destinations, they’re ideal for off-road adventures and challenging routes such as Brownsberg.

Please note: These buses do not have air conditioning.

  • Remote route access
  • clean & well-maintained buses
  • professional drivers
